Included on every plan: applications and screening, document library, Q&A with entrepreneurs, AngelAI chat, cross-document consistency analysis, EIS/SEIS reference content, email notifications, and the full deal workflow through to close.

All paid plans also incur a **£50 charge per project that reaches the Due Diligence stage**. The charge only applies when a pitch has been made, angels have expressed interest, and a lead investor has been appointed. Screened-and-declined projects incur no per-project fee.

## Annual billing

All plans are available monthly or annually. **Annual billing gives you 2 months free** — the annual price is ten times the monthly price.

## Adding a payment card

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Add payment card**
3. Enter your card details in the secure Stripe payment form
4. Click **Save card**

Your card details are stored securely by Stripe — AngelHub never stores card numbers directly.

## The DD project charge

When a project moves to Due Diligence, AngelHub shows you the project name, the £50 charge, and the last 4 digits of your card on file. Click **Accept** to proceed or **Decline** to keep the project at its current stage.

<Note>
If no card is on file when a project reaches DD, you will be prompted to add one before accepting the charge.
</Note>

## Viewing your billing history

All charges are listed in **Settings → Billing → Charge history**, including monthly subscription charges and DD project charges with their status.

## Upgrading or downgrading

Go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**. Changes take effect immediately. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

<Warning>
If you downgrade to Free, projects beyond the 3-project limit become read-only. No data is deleted — upgrade again at any time to restore full access.
</Warning>

## Cancelling

Go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. Your group moves to the Free plan at the end of your current billing period. Your data is available for export for 90 days after cancellation.
