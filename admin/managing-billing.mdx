Included on every plan: applications and screening, document library, Q&A with entrepreneurs, AngelAI chat, cross-document consistency analysis, EIS/SEIS reference content, email notifications, and the full deal workflow through to close.

**Due diligence is available on every plan** at a flat £50 per project that reaches DD. The charge applies only when a pitch has been made, angels have expressed interest, and a lead investor has been appointed. Screened-and-declined projects incur no per-project fee.

## Annual billing

Annual subscriptions are ten times the monthly price (Pro £990/yr, Scale £1,990/yr), giving two months free. Billing period — monthly or annual — is set when you start your subscription and is shown in your billing screen.

## The group billing screen

Go to **Admin → Billing** to see:

- Your current plan, billing period, and the date it started
- The payment card on file (brand, last four digits, expiry) with an **Add card** / **Replace card** button
- What you will be charged — the £50 DD project fee and, for Scale groups, any extra-administrator charges
- Your full charge history, newest first

No card details ever reach AngelHub's servers — cards are stored securely with Stripe.

## Adding or replacing a payment card

From the billing screen, click **Add card** or **Replace card** and enter your card details in the secure Stripe form. Your card is also captured automatically the first time a project moves to DD if no card is on file.

## The DD project charge

When a project moves to Due Diligence, AngelHub shows you the project name, the £50 charge, and the last four digits of your card. Click **Accept** to proceed or **Decline** to leave the project at its current stage.

<Note>
If no card is on file, you will be prompted to add one before accepting the charge. This applies on all plans including Free.
</Note>

## Extra Scale administrators

Scale includes 3 admins. Additional admins beyond 3 are charged at £10/month or £100/year each. Extra admin charges take effect from the first day of the following calendar month — no mid-month proration. The billing screen shows current admins, billable extras, any pending next-month quantity, and the effective date.

## Upgrading or downgrading

Contact AngelHub to change your plan. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

<Warning>
If you downgrade to Free, projects beyond the 3-project limit become read-only. No data is deleted — upgrade again at any time to restore full access.
</Warning>

## Cancelling

Contact AngelHub to cancel. Your group moves to the Free plan at the end of your current billing period. Your data is available for export for 90 days after cancellation.
