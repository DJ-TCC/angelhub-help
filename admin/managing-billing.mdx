## Plans and pricing

AngelHub has three plans:

| Plan | Monthly fee | Concurrent projects | Angels per deal | Admins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | £0 | 3 | 5 | 1 |
| **Pro** | £99/month | 10 | Unlimited | 1 |
| **Scale** | £199/month | Unlimited | Unlimited | 3 |

All paid plans also incur a **£50 charge per project that reaches the Due Diligence stage**. This charge is applied at the point the project moves to DD and must be accepted before the project proceeds.

## Adding a payment card

To use the Pro or Scale plan, or to accept DD project charges, you need a payment card on file.

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Add payment card**
3. Enter your card details in the secure Stripe payment form
4. Click **Save card**

Your card details are stored securely by Stripe — AngelHub never stores card numbers directly.

## The DD project charge

When a Project Lead or Admin attempts to move a project to the Due Diligence stage, AngelHub will:

1. Show you the project name and the £50 charge
2. Show the last 4 digits of your card on file
3. Ask you to **Accept** or **Decline**

If you accept, the charge is applied immediately and the project moves to DD. If you decline, the project stays at its current stage.

<Note>
If no card is on file when a project reaches DD, you will be prompted to add one before you can accept the charge.
</Note>

## Viewing your billing history

All charges are listed in **Settings → Billing → Charge history**, including:

- Monthly subscription charges
- DD project charges (showing the project name and date)
- The status of each charge (successful, pending, or failed)

## Upgrading or downgrading your plan

To change your plan, go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**. Changes take effect immediately.

**Upgrading** — you are charged the difference for the remainder of the current billing month, then the full new amount from the next billing date.

**Downgrading** — takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You retain access to your current plan's features until then.

<Warning>
If you downgrade from Scale or Pro to Free, any projects beyond the Free plan's 3-project limit will become read-only. No data is deleted — you can upgrade again at any time to restore full access.
</Warning>

## Cancelling

To cancel, go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. Your group moves to the Free plan at the end of your current billing period. All your data is retained.
