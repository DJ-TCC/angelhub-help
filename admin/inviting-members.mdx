title: Inviting members
description: How to invite angels, assessors, project leads, and additional admins to your group.
---

AngelHub has five roles. Each role sees a different view of the platform and has access to different features.

## The five roles

| Role | What they do | Active stages |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Manages the group — members, settings, billing | All stages |
| **Assessor** | Screens applications for pitch readiness | Created → Screening → Pitching |
| **Project Lead** | Leads due diligence on a specific deal | Pitching → DD → Completion |
| **Angel (Member)** | Reviews deals, votes, uses AngelAI | Pitching → DD → Completion |
| **Entrepreneur** | Submits and tracks their own application | Their application only |

Entrepreneurs are not invited — they apply through your public application link.

## Inviting someone

1. Go to **Members → Invite member**
2. Enter their email address
3. Select their role
4. Click **Send invitation**

They'll receive an email with a link to create their account. The invitation expires after 7 days.

<Note>
On the **Free plan**, angels per deal are limited to 5 and only 1 admin is included. **Pro** includes 2 admins and unlimited angels per deal. **Scale** includes 3 admins, with additional admins available at £10/month each.
</Note>

## Assigning a Project Lead to a deal

Project Leads are assigned per project, not per group. Once a project reaches the Pitching stage, go to the project and select **Assign Project Lead** from the project menu.

## Managing existing members

From the **Members** list you can:
- **Resend an invitation** — for members who haven't accepted yet
- **Change a member's role** — takes effect immediately
- **Remove a member** — they lose access immediately. Historical activity is retained for audit purposes.

## Adding a second admin

On the **Pro plan**, you can have up to 2 admins. On **Scale**, up to 3. Additional admins beyond 3 on Scale are available at £10/month each.

<Warning>
Only invite people as admins who genuinely need full access to group settings and member data.
</Warning>
