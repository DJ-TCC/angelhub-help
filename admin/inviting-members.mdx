---
title: Inviting members
description: How to invite angels, assessors, project leads, and additional admins to your group.
---

AngelHub has five roles. Each role sees a different view of the platform and has access to different features. You assign a role when you send an invitation.

## The five roles

| Role | What they do | Active stages |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Manages the group — members, settings, billing | All stages |
| **Assessor** | Screens applications for pitch readiness | Created → Screening → Pitching |
| **Project Lead** | Leads due diligence on a specific deal | Pitching → DD → Completion |
| **Angel (Member)** | Reviews deals, votes, uses AngelAI | Pitching → DD → Completion |
| **Entrepreneur** | Submits and tracks their own application | Their application only |

Entrepreneurs are not invited — they apply through your public application link. All other roles are invited by an admin.

## Inviting someone

1. Go to **Members → Invite member**
2. Enter their email address
3. Select their role
4. Click **Send invitation**

They'll receive an email with a link to create their account. The invitation expires after 7 days — you can resend it from the Members list if needed.

<Note>
On the **Free plan**, angels per deal are limited to 5. On **Pro and Scale**, there is no limit. Admin accounts are limited to 1 on Free and Pro, and 3 on Scale.
</Note>

## Assigning a Project Lead to a deal

Project Leads are assigned per project, not per group. Once a project reaches the Pitching stage, go to the project and select **Assign Project Lead** from the project menu. You can reassign a Project Lead at any time.

Any angel member can be assigned as Project Lead on a deal — they don't need a separate invitation.

## Managing existing members

From the **Members** list you can:
- **Resend an invitation** — for members who haven't accepted yet
- **Change a member's role** — takes effect immediately
- **Remove a member** — they lose access to the group immediately. Their historical activity (notes, votes, assessments) is retained for audit purposes but is no longer editable.

## Adding a second or third admin

On the **Scale plan**, you can have up to 3 admins. Additional admins beyond 3 are available at £10/month each.

To add an admin, invite them with the **Admin** role. Admins have full access to all group settings, all projects, and all member data.

<Warning>
Only invite people as admins who genuinely need full access to group settings and member data. For most angels, the Member role is appropriate.
</Warning>
