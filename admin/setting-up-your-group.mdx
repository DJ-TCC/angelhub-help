This guide walks you through the complete first-time setup of your angel group on AngelHub. It should take around 20 minutes.

## Before you start

You'll need:
- Your group's name and a brief description
- A list of your current members and their email addresses
- Your group's logo (optional but recommended)

## Step 1 — Complete your group profile

After signing in for the first time, you'll be prompted to complete your group profile. Fill in:

- **Group name** — the name that appears throughout the platform
- **Description** — a short summary of your group's focus and geography (2–3 sentences)
- **Logo** — upload a PNG or SVG file. Recommended size: 400×400px minimum

Your group profile is visible to entrepreneurs whose applications you accept, so keep the description professional.

## Step 2 — Configure your pipeline stages

AngelHub ships with a default six-stage pipeline:

1. Created
2. Screening
3. Pitching
4. Due Diligence
5. Closing
6. Completed

If your group uses different stage names, you can rename them in **Settings → Pipeline**. The order and number of stages cannot be changed on the Free plan. Scale plan groups can customise stages fully.

<Note>
Renaming a stage affects all projects in your group. If you have live projects, rename stages before inviting members to avoid confusion.
</Note>

## Step 3 — Set up your application form

The default application form captures the information most angel groups need: company name, sector, stage, funding amount sought, brief description, and website.

On the **Scale plan**, you can customise the form fields to match your group's specific requirements. Go to **Settings → Application form** to add, remove, or reorder fields.

If you're on the Free or Pro plan, the default form is active immediately — no setup required.

## Step 4 — Invite your members

See the [Inviting members](/admin/inviting-members) guide for the full process. At minimum, invite the members who will be acting as Assessors before you start receiving applications.

## Step 5 — Test with a dummy application

Before going live, submit a test application through your public application link to confirm the form works and that you receive the notification email. You can delete the test application afterwards.

Your public application link is shown in **Settings → Application form → Share link**.

## Step 6 — Set up your billing

If you're on the Pro or Scale plan, add a payment card in **Settings → Billing**. This card is used for the £50 per-project Due Diligence charge when a project reaches the DD stage.

See [Managing billing](/admin/managing-billing) for full details.

## You're ready

Once you've completed these steps, your group is live. Share the application link with entrepreneurs and invite your angels to join.

<Tip>
Bookmark the AngelHub admin dashboard. You'll be here often — it shows all active projects, member activity, and any items needing your attention.
</Tip>
