This guide walks you through the complete first-time setup of your angel group on AngelHub. It should take around 20 minutes.

## Before you start

You'll need:
- Your group's name and a brief description
- A list of your current members and their email addresses
- Your group's logo (optional but recommended)

## Step 1 — Complete your group profile

After signing in for the first time, you'll be prompted to complete your group profile. Fill in:

- **Group name** — the name that appears throughout the platform
- **Description** — a short summary of your group's focus and geography (2–3 sentences)
- **Logo** — upload a PNG or SVG file. Recommended size: 400×400px minimum

## Step 2 — Configure your pipeline stages

AngelHub ships with a default six-stage pipeline: Created, Screening, Pitching, Due Diligence, Closing, Completed.

If your group uses different stage names, you can rename them in **Settings → Pipeline**.

<Note>
Renaming a stage affects all projects in your group. If you have live projects, rename stages before inviting members to avoid confusion.
</Note>

## Step 3 — Set up your application form

The default application form captures the information most angel groups need. On the **Scale plan**, you can customise the form fields in **Settings → Application form**.

## Step 4 — Invite your members

See the [Inviting members](/admin/inviting-members) guide. At minimum, invite Assessors before you start receiving applications.

## Step 5 — Set up your sending email address

AngelHub sends group emails from an address you configure. Free and Pro plans include one sending address (e.g. `yourgroupname@angelhub.net`). Scale includes five sending addresses and the ability to send from your own domain.

Go to **Settings → Email** to configure your sending address.

## Step 6 — Test with a dummy application

Submit a test application through your public application link to confirm the form works. Your public link is shown in **Settings → Application form → Share link**.

## Step 7 — Set up your billing

If you're on Pro or Scale, add a payment card in **Settings → Billing**. See [Managing billing](/admin/managing-billing) for full details.

## You're ready

Share the application link with entrepreneurs and invite your angels to join.

<Tip>
Bookmark the AngelHub admin dashboard — it shows all active projects, member activity, and items needing your attention.
</Tip>
