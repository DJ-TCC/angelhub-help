## What angel members do

As an angel member, you are an investor in the group. Your role in AngelHub is to review deals that have passed screening and pitching, contribute to due diligence, vote on investment decisions, and — if the group invests — become a shareholder in the company.

You are not responsible for running the DD process. That is the Project Lead's job. Your role is to engage with the deals that interest you, contribute your expertise where it is relevant, and make informed voting decisions.

## When you are active

Angel members are active from the **Pitching** stage onwards:

- **Pitching** — attending pitch events, forming an initial view
- **Due Diligence** — reviewing DD findings, contributing to work packages, using AngelAI
- **Closing** — confirming your investment commitment
- **Completed** — the investment is recorded in the group portfolio

You do not have access to the Screening stage — applications are filtered by Assessors before they reach you.

## What you can see and do

In AngelHub you can:

- View all projects in the Pitching, DD, Closing, and Completed stages
- Read the entrepreneur's submission and all Q&A history
- Use AngelAI to ask questions about a deal
- View the coverage map and work package findings
- Add private notes on a project
- Vote on investment decisions
- Read notes shared with you by the Project Lead (Scale plan)

You cannot:

- Move projects between stages
- Access group settings or billing
- See projects that are still in Screening

## Your relationship with the Project Lead

The Project Lead coordinates the DD process and presents the group recommendation. Your job is to engage with their work — read their updates, ask questions, contribute your expertise to specific work packages if invited, and form your own view.

If you have relevant expertise for a particular deal — sector knowledge, legal experience, technical background — tell the Project Lead. They may ask you to lead a specific work package, which means investigating that area and reporting your findings back to the group.

## Making your investment decision

Your investment decision is your own. AngelHub provides the tools and information to help you make it well — the DD findings, AngelAI, the WNTBB summary — but the decision to invest, and how much, is entirely yours.

Each angel invests individually. You are not bound by the group's majority view. If the group recommends investing but you have reservations, you can decline to participate. If the group decides not to invest but you want to, you are free to pursue the investment independently — though this is rare.

See [Voting on a deal](/angels/voting-on-a-deal) for how the formal decision process works in AngelHub.
