## AngelAI for angel members

AngelAI is available to all angel members on active projects. It draws on the AngelHub knowledge base — 33 documents covering every aspect of angel investment — to help you think through deals more clearly.

As an angel member, you are not running the DD process, but you are forming your own investment view. AngelAI helps you do that more rigorously.

## What to ask AngelAI

**Understanding the business**

"This company is a B2B SaaS business selling to NHS trusts. What should I understand about this sector before I assess the deal?"

"The company has £120k ARR and is growing 15% month-on-month. Is that strong for a business at this stage?"

**Challenging the DD findings**

"The Project Lead has assessed the team as strong, but I notice neither founder has commercial sales experience. Should I be concerned about this?"

"The financial model projects 40% gross margin improving to 65% in year three. Is that trajectory credible for a SaaS business?"

**Understanding the terms**

"What does a 20% discount on an ASA mean for my effective valuation?"

"The term sheet includes a weighted average anti-dilution clause. How does that protect me if there's a down round?"

**EIS and tax**

"Does this company's structure — incorporated 4 years ago, no prior EIS investment — affect my EIS eligibility?"

"When does my three-year EIS hold period start if the company used an Advance Subscription Agreement?"

**Making your decision**

"Based on what I've read, the key risks seem to be X, Y, and Z. How should I weigh these against the upside?"

"Help me think through whether the valuation is fair given the company's current position."

## How to read AngelAI responses

Each response shows:

- **Source badges** — the knowledge base documents AngelAI drew on. These tell you the basis for the answer.
- **Confidence level** — high means the knowledge base directly addresses your question; low means AngelAI is reasoning more generally and you should treat the answer as a starting point.
- **Suggested follow-up** — a next question to deepen your thinking.

If AngelAI gives a low-confidence response, or if the question has significant financial or legal implications, take the answer as a direction to explore rather than a conclusion.

## What AngelAI will not do

AngelAI will not tell you whether to invest. It will help you think through the question — surfacing relevant frameworks, pointing out considerations you may have missed, stress-testing your assumptions — but the decision is yours.

It will also not give you personal legal or tax advice. For questions about your specific EIS/SEIS position, your liability as a director, or your personal tax situation, speak to a qualified adviser.

## Free plan AI usage

On the Free plan, AngelAI responses are limited to 50 per month across the whole group. If you see a message that the monthly limit has been reached, speak to your group admin about upgrading to Pro or Scale for unlimited access.
