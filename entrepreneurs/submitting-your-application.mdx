## Before you apply

Read the angel group's investment criteria carefully before submitting. Most groups have a preferred stage, sector, and geography. Applying to a group that does not invest in your sector or at your stage wastes your time and theirs.

If you are not sure whether your company is a fit, email the group directly to ask before submitting a formal application.

## Completing the application form

The application form asks for information about your business. Take your time — a well-written application significantly increases your chances of being invited to pitch.

**Company basics**
Your company name, website, incorporation date, and registered address. Make sure these are accurate — they will be verified if you reach due diligence.

**The problem and your solution**
Describe the problem you are solving and how your product or service solves it. Be specific and concrete. Avoid jargon. A clear, simple explanation of a real problem is far more compelling than technical language describing a feature.

**Your market**
Describe the market you are addressing. Give a realistic estimate of the market size — not the global total addressable market, but the specific segment you are targeting in the near term. Include the source of your market size estimate.

**Traction**
What have you achieved so far? Include paying customers, revenue, user numbers, letters of intent, partnerships, or any other evidence that your market responds to what you are building. Be honest about where you are — investors see hundreds of applications and will verify what you claim.

**The team**
Describe the founding team and any key hires. Include relevant experience and why this team is well-placed to build this business. If there are gaps in the team, acknowledge them and describe your plan to fill them.

**The funding ask**
How much are you raising, at what valuation, and what will you use the funds for? Be specific about use of funds — "hiring, product, and marketing" is too vague. Say how many people you will hire, in what roles, and what product milestones you will achieve.

**EIS/SEIS**
Indicate whether you have EIS or SEIS advance assurance from HMRC, or whether you believe you qualify. This is important to most UK angel groups — if you are unsure, say so and explain your situation.

## Attaching documents

Most application forms allow you to attach supporting documents. At minimum, attach:

- Your pitch deck (PDF, maximum 20MB)
- Your most recent financial model or projections (if available)

Do not attach lengthy business plans or technical documents at the application stage. Keep it concise — you will have the opportunity to share more detail if you are invited to pitch.

## Submitting

When you are satisfied with your application, click **Submit**. You will receive a confirmation email.

Your application is now in the angel group's pipeline at the **Created** stage. An Assessor will review it and decide whether to invite you to pitch.

<Note>
You cannot edit your application after submitting. If you realise you have made a significant error, email the group directly to explain.
</Note>
