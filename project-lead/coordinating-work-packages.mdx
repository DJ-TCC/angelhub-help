## Distributing the work

A solo Project Lead doing all 8 work packages alone is a slow and narrow DD. The best DD processes distribute the work across angels with relevant expertise — a lawyer on WP7, a finance professional on WP6, a sector expert on WP4.

When you set up the DD, identify which angels in your group have relevant skills for each work package and approach them directly. Most angels are willing to contribute a few hours to a specific area they know well.

**How to assign work packages in AngelHub:**

1. Open the project in DD
2. Go to the **Work packages** tab
3. Select a work package and assign it to a member of the group
4. The assigned angel receives a notification and can add their findings directly to the work package

The assigned angel sees the work package framework, the entrepreneur's submission, and any documents in the data room relevant to their area. They can also use AngelAI within the work package context.

## Tracking progress

The coverage map gives you a real-time view of DD progress across all 11 topics. Check it at the start of each week and follow up with assigned angels on any areas showing red (not yet addressed).

A weekly check-in message to the group — even just a brief note via the project's shared notes — keeps momentum going and surfaces blockers early.

## The WNTBB framework

At the end of DD, you present your recommendation to the group using the **What Needs to Be Believed (WNTBB)** framework. This is the most important thing you will produce as Project Lead.

The WNTBB framework asks: what specific things need to be true for this investment to succeed, and what is the evidence that those things are or will be true?

### Step 1 — Identify the critical assumptions

Every investment thesis rests on 3–6 specific assumptions that are:
- **Material** — if the assumption is wrong, the investment fails
- **Uncertain** — not yet proven, but testable
- **Specific** — not "the market is large" but "there are at least 5,000 UK businesses who will pay £500/month for this"

Assumptions typically fall into five categories: **Team**, **Market**, **Product**, **Commercial**, and **Financial**.

### Step 2 — Assess the evidence for each assumption

For each assumption, identify:
- What evidence from DD supports it being true
- What evidence suggests it might not be true
- What additional information would increase your confidence

### Step 3 — Assign a verdict to each assumption

- **Believed** — the evidence is sufficient; invest on this assumption
- **Believed with reservation** — broadly supported but a specific gap remains; document the reservation
- **Not yet believed** — not enough evidence; more DD needed before deciding
- **Not believed** — the evidence is inconsistent with the assumption being true

### Step 4 — Reach the overall recommendation

- All believed or believed with reservation → **recommend investing**
- One or more not yet believed → **defer; define specific additional DD required**
- One or more not believed on a material assumption → **decline or reprice**

### Presenting to the group

Structure your group presentation around the WNTBB:

1. Open with the three to six assumptions — state them clearly
2. For each: the evidence found, the verdict, and any reservation
3. Overall recommendation with brief rationale
4. Any specific conditions you would attach to an investment (warranties, milestones, valuation adjustment)

A well-structured WNTBB presentation takes 15–20 minutes to present and enables a focused group discussion. The group should be able to challenge your verdicts on individual assumptions rather than debating the investment in the abstract.

<Tip>
Use AngelAI to help you build the WNTBB. Describe the DD findings and ask: "Help me identify the key assumptions for this investment and structure them as a WNTBB assessment." It will draw on the wntbb1 framework document and help you think through each assumption systematically.
</Tip>

## After the recommendation

**If the group decides to invest** — move to the Closing stage and begin the legal process. See your admin for the legal documentation workflow.

**If the group decides to defer** — document the specific additional information or events required before the decision can be revisited. Set a review date and communicate it to the entrepreneur.

**If the group decides to decline** — record the reason in the project and move to Archived. Communicate the decision to the entrepreneur promptly and professionally.
