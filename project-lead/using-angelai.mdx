## What AngelAI is

AngelAI is your AI due diligence assistant. It is built into every project and draws on the AngelHub knowledge base — a set of 33 documents written by experienced UK angel investors covering every aspect of angel investment due diligence.

AngelAI is a thinking partner, not a decision-maker. It helps you surface the right questions, apply the right frameworks, and understand the implications of what you find. The judgment is always yours.

## How AngelAI works

When you ask AngelAI a question, it:

1. Classifies what kind of question it is (valuation, DD process, legal, sector-specific, etc.)
2. Retrieves the most relevant content from the knowledge base
3. Generates a response grounded in that content
4. Cites the source documents it drew on (shown as badges below the response)
5. Signals its confidence level — high, medium, or low
6. Suggests a follow-up question to keep your thinking moving

If AngelAI cannot find a relevant answer in the knowledge base, it will tell you — it will not make something up.

## What to ask AngelAI during DD

AngelAI is most useful when you ask it specific, contextual questions. Here are the most valuable uses during a live DD:

**Work package guidance**
"What are the key questions I should be asking about this company's IP ownership?"
"What does a credible B2B SaaS financial model look like at seed stage?"
"What red flags should I look for when reviewing a university spinout?"

**Valuation sense-checking**
"The company is asking for a £3m pre-money valuation with £80k ARR. How should I think about this?"
"What valuation methodology is appropriate for a pre-revenue deep tech company?"

**Legal and structural questions**
"What should I look for in a disclosure letter?"
"What are the EIS eligibility requirements for a SaaS company?"
"What does good leaver / bad leaver mean and why does it matter?"

**Synthesising DD findings**
"I've found the following during DD: [summarise findings]. What are the key risks I should be focused on?"
"Help me structure a WNTBB assessment for this investment."

**Sector-specific questions**
"What metrics matter most for a marketplace business at this stage?"
"What does good retention look like for a consumer D2C brand?"

## Reading AngelAI responses

Every AngelAI response shows:

- **Source badges** — the knowledge base documents the response drew on (e.g. `vg1`, `wp10`, `rf1`). Click a badge to see the source document.
- **Confidence level** — high (knowledge base directly addresses this), medium (partial coverage), or low (limited knowledge base coverage, more general reasoning applied)
- **Suggested follow-up** — a question AngelAI recommends asking next

If the confidence is low, treat the response as a starting point for your own research rather than a definitive answer.

## What AngelAI does not do

AngelAI does not:
- Give you a recommendation on whether to invest
- Give legal advice — it will tell you what questions to ask a solicitor, not what the legal answer is
- Give financial advice — it will explain EIS/SEIS mechanics, not tell you what your tax position will be
- Access the internet or any external sources — it works only from the AngelHub knowledge base and the context of the current project

If you ask AngelAI something outside its scope, it will say so clearly and suggest where to find the right answer.

## Tips for better results

**Be specific.** "Tell me about EIS" produces a generic response. "Does this company's structure — a holding company with a trading subsidiary incorporated 3 years ago — affect EIS eligibility?" produces a targeted one.

**Provide context.** AngelAI knows which project you're in, but it doesn't know what you've found in DD unless you tell it. Share relevant details in your question: "The founders have a background in academic research with no prior commercial experience. What should I be probing on the team work package?"

**Use it iteratively.** Don't expect a single question to produce a complete DD answer. Use the suggested follow-up questions to go deeper. A good DD conversation with AngelAI typically runs 5–10 exchanges on a specific topic.

**Save useful responses.** On Scale plan, you can save AI responses to your private notes for reference later in the process or when presenting to the group.
