## What AngelAI is

AngelAI is your AI due diligence assistant. It draws on the AngelHub knowledge base — a set of documents written by experienced UK angel investors covering every aspect of angel investment due diligence.

AngelAI is a thinking partner, not a decision-maker. It helps you surface the right questions, apply the right frameworks, and understand the implications of what you find. The judgment is always yours.

## AI response limits by plan

| Plan | AI responses per month |
|---|---|
| Free | 50 |
| Pro | 150 |
| Scale | Unlimited |

The limit is shared across the whole group. If you reach your monthly limit, AngelAI will tell you. The limit resets on the first of each calendar month.

## What to ask AngelAI during DD

**Guidance on specific DD areas**
- "What are the key questions I should be asking about this company's IP ownership?"
- "What does a credible B2B SaaS financial model look like at seed stage?"
- "What red flags should I look for when reviewing a university spinout?"

**Valuation sense-checking**
- "The company is asking for a £3m pre-money valuation with £80k ARR. How should I think about this?"
- "What valuation methodology is appropriate for a pre-revenue deep tech company?"

**Legal and structural questions**
- "What should I look for in a disclosure letter?"
- "What are the EIS eligibility requirements for a SaaS company?"

**Synthesising DD findings**
- "Help me structure a WNTBB assessment for this investment."
- "I've found the following during DD: [summarise]. What are the key risks I should be focused on?"

## Reading AngelAI responses

Every response shows a confidence level and a suggested follow-up question.

<Note>
**Source citations** — showing which knowledge base document each finding came from — are available on the Scale plan only. On Free and Pro, you see the AI finding without the source attribution.
</Note>

## What AngelAI does not do

- Give you a recommendation on whether to invest
- Give legal advice — it tells you what questions to ask a solicitor
- Give financial advice — it explains EIS/SEIS mechanics, not your personal tax position
- Access the internet or external sources

## Tips for better results

**Be specific.** "Tell me about EIS" produces a generic response. "Does this company's holding structure affect EIS eligibility?" produces a targeted one.

**Provide context.** Share relevant details: "The founders have academic backgrounds with no prior commercial experience. What should I focus on when assessing the team?"

**Use it iteratively.** A good DD conversation with AngelAI typically runs 5–10 exchanges on a specific topic.
