## The Project Lead's job

The Project Lead is the angel investor who takes primary responsibility for a deal from the pitch stage through to completion. They coordinate due diligence, communicate with the entrepreneur, present findings to the group, and manage the process through to investment or decline.

The Project Lead is not a solo decision-maker. They are a coordinator and synthesiser — their job is to organise the group's collective assessment and present it in a way that enables a clear investment decision.

## When Project Leads are active

Project Leads are assigned when a project moves from Screening to Pitching. They are active across:

- **Pitching** — attending the pitch, coordinating post-pitch discussion, deciding whether to proceed to DD
- **Due Diligence** — running the full DD process across 8 work packages, using AngelAI, coordinating work package owners
- **Closing** — managing the legal and completion process alongside the group admin
- **Completed** — recording the final investment details and handing the project to the portfolio

## What Project Leads can see and do

Project Leads have full access to their assigned projects, including:

- The entrepreneur's full submission and all Q&A history
- The AngelAI chat (with full DD context)
- The coverage map and all 8 work packages
- Private notes and note sharing with other angels on the deal
- The WNTBB (What Needs to Be Believed) assessment framework
- The ability to move the project between stages
- Cross-document consistency analysis (Scale plan)
- Source citations on AI findings (Scale plan)

Project Leads do not have access to group settings, billing, or other projects they are not assigned to (unless they are also an admin).

## The Project Lead and the group

The Project Lead is accountable to the group. At the end of due diligence, they present a structured recommendation using the WNTBB framework — setting out the key assumptions that need to be true for the investment to succeed, the evidence for and against each, and a clear recommendation to invest, defer, or decline.

This presentation is the culmination of the DD process. A well-prepared WNTBB summary makes the group discussion focused and efficient.

See [Preparing your WNTBB summary](/project-lead/coordinating-work-packages) for guidance on how to structure this.

## Typical Project Lead workflow

1. Assigned to the project at the Pitching stage
2. Attend or coordinate the pitch event
3. Initiate due diligence — move the project to DD (a £50 charge applies)
4. Run the DD process across 8 work packages using AngelAI and the coverage map
5. Coordinate work package owners from the angel group
6. Prepare and present the WNTBB summary to the group
7. Manage the closing process if the group decides to invest

See [Taking on a project](/project-lead/taking-on-a-project) for the step-by-step process from pitch to DD.
