## Being assigned as Project Lead

You become Project Lead when an admin assigns you at the Pitching stage. You'll receive an email notification. Before the pitch, read the entrepreneur's full submission and the Assessor's screening notes in the **Submission** and **Screening** tabs.

## At the pitch

Your job at the pitch event is to listen actively and identify the questions that need answering in DD. During or immediately after the pitch, note:
- What impressed you about the team
- What was unclear or unconvincing
- What the entrepreneur said that needs verifying
- What was not covered that needs to be

<Note>
**Private notes** are available on Pro and Scale plans. On the Free plan, use the project Q&A or your own notes outside the platform to capture your observations.
</Note>

<Tip>
Use AngelAI after the pitch. Describe what you heard and ask: "Based on this, what are the key things I need to investigate in due diligence?"
</Tip>

## Deciding whether to proceed to DD

After the pitch, the group needs to decide: proceed to due diligence, or decline. The key question is not "do we want to invest?" — it is "do we know enough to justify the time and cost of due diligence?"

## Moving to Due Diligence

When the group agrees to proceed:

1. Open the project in AngelHub
2. Click **Move to Due Diligence**
3. A £50 charge confirmation appears — accept to proceed
4. The project moves to DD and the full DD toolkit becomes available

<Note>
The £50 DD charge applies only when a pitch has been made, angels have expressed interest, and a lead investor has been appointed. Projects screened and declined before a pitch incur no per-project fee. The charge is per project, not per investor.
</Note>

## Setting up the DD process

Once in DD:
1. **Review the coverage map** — understand the scope of work across the 11 DD topics
2. **Agree responsibilities with your angels** — contact relevant angels directly to agree who will cover which area
3. **Contact the entrepreneur** — let them know DD has started and what you'll need
4. **Set a target timeline** — typical angel DD runs 6–8 weeks

See [Running a DD](/project-lead/running-a-dd) for the full process.
