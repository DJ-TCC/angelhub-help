## Being assigned as Project Lead

You become Project Lead when an admin assigns you to a project at the Pitching stage. You'll receive an email notification. The project will appear in your **Projects** list with your name shown as Project Lead.

Before the pitch, read the entrepreneur's full submission and the Assessor's screening notes. These are in the **Submission** and **Screening** tabs of the project. The Assessor's notes will tell you what they found compelling, what questions they had, and any concerns worth investigating further in DD.

## At the pitch

Your job at the pitch event is to listen actively and identify the questions that need answering in DD — not to make a decision. During or immediately after the pitch, note:

- What impressed you about the team
- What was unclear or unconvincing
- What the entrepreneur said that needs verifying
- What was not covered that needs to be

These notes form the starting point for your DD work packages. Add them to the **Private notes** section of the project immediately after the pitch while they are fresh.

<Tip>
Use AngelAI after the pitch. Describe what you heard and ask it: "Based on this, what are the key things I need to investigate in due diligence?" It will help you structure your initial DD plan against the work package framework.
</Tip>

## Deciding whether to proceed to DD

After the pitch, the group needs to make a preliminary decision: proceed to due diligence, or decline.

This decision is typically made at the pitch meeting or in a follow-up discussion. As Project Lead, your role is to facilitate this discussion and form a clear view to present to the group.

The key question is not "do we want to invest?" — it is "do we know enough to justify the time and cost of due diligence?" DD is an investment of time from multiple angels. It should only be initiated if the group believes there is a genuine possibility of investment.

## Moving to Due Diligence

When the group agrees to proceed:

1. Open the project in AngelHub
2. Click **Move to Due Diligence** in the project header
3. A charge confirmation will appear — £50 will be charged to the group's card on file
4. Click **Accept** to confirm
5. The project moves to the DD stage and the full DD toolkit becomes available

<Note>
The £50 DD charge is applied per project, not per investor. It covers AngelAI-assisted DD support for the full duration of the project in DD. If you decline the charge, the project stays at the Pitching stage.
</Note>

## Setting up the DD process

Once the project is in DD, your first tasks are:

1. **Review the coverage map** — the 11-topic grid shows what DD areas need to be addressed. Start here to understand the scope of work.
2. **Assign work packages** — identify which angels in the group will take responsibility for each work package area. You can do this informally or via the work package assignments in the project.
3. **Contact the entrepreneur** — let them know DD has started, what you'll need from them, and the expected timeline. Use the Q&A tool for formal requests.
4. **Set a target timeline** — a typical angel DD runs 6–8 weeks. Set a target completion date and share it with the group.

See [Running a DD](/project-lead/running-a-dd) for the full DD process.
