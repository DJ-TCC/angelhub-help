---
title: Reviewing an application
description: A step-by-step guide to reviewing an entrepreneur's application as an Assessor.
---

## Step 1 — Open the application

When a new application is submitted, you'll receive an email notification. Log in and go to **Projects** — new applications appear at the top of the **Created** stage.

Click the application to open it. You'll see the entrepreneur's full submission, including their answers to the application form questions, any attached documents, and their company website.

## Step 2 — Move to Screening

Once you start your review, move the application to **Screening**. This signals to other admins and assessors that someone is actively reviewing it and prevents duplicated effort.

Click **Move to Screening** in the project header.

## Step 3 — Read the full submission

Before using any assessment tools, read the entrepreneur's submission in full without interruption. Form an initial view — does this feel like a business that is ready to pitch? What stands out positively? What is unclear or concerning?

This first read gives you a gut-level impression that is worth noting before you start the structured assessment.

## Step 4 — Work through the screening framework

AngelHub's screening framework assesses the application across 8 dimensions. Go to the **Screening** tab within the project to see the framework.

For each dimension, record your assessment and any notes. See [The screening framework](/assessor/screening-framework) for guidance on what to look for in each dimension.

## Step 5 — Ask clarifying questions

If the submission is incomplete or raises specific questions, use the **Q&A** tool to ask the entrepreneur directly. Questions are sent by email and the entrepreneur responds through their AngelHub portal.

Keep questions focused — one to three questions per round is usually appropriate. A long list of questions before a pitch can feel off-putting to an entrepreneur.

<Tip>
Use AngelAI to help you frame questions. Ask it: "What questions should I ask an entrepreneur in [sector] about their go-to-market strategy?" It will draw on the AngelHub knowledge base to suggest relevant lines of inquiry.
</Tip>

## Step 6 — Make your decision

Once you have completed the screening assessment and received any clarifying answers, make your decision:

**Move to Pitching** — the application is sufficiently developed and the company is ready to present to the group. Click **Move to Pitching** in the project header. You will be prompted to assign a Project Lead.

**Decline** — the application is not ready or not suitable. Click **Decline** in the project menu and record a brief reason. The entrepreneur is not automatically notified — you can choose whether and how to communicate the decision.

**Request more information** — if you need more from the entrepreneur before deciding, continue the Q&A process. Leave the project in Screening until you have what you need.

## What happens next

Once a project moves to Pitching, a Project Lead takes over responsibility for the deal. The Assessor's role on this application is largely complete, though you may be asked to provide context to the Project Lead if needed.
