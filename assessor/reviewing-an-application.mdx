---
title: Reviewing an application
description: A step-by-step guide to reviewing an entrepreneur's application as an Assessor.
---

## Step 1 — Open the application

When a new application is submitted, you'll receive an email notification. Go to **Projects** — new applications appear at the top of the **Created** stage.

## Step 2 — Move to Screening

Once you start your review, move the application to **Screening**. This signals to others that someone is actively reviewing it and prevents duplicated effort.

## Step 3 — Read the full submission

Before using any assessment tools, read the entrepreneur's submission in full without interruption. Form an initial view — does this feel like a business that is ready to pitch?

## Step 4 — Work through the screening framework

Go to the **Screening** tab within the project. For each of the 8 dimensions, record your assessment and any notes. See [The screening framework](/assessor/screening-framework) for guidance.

## Step 5 — Generate a lead review (Scale plan)

On the **Scale plan**, you can generate an AI-powered lead review of the entrepreneur's submission. This gives you a structured assessment of the application across the key screening dimensions, saving significant time on initial review.

<Note>
Lead review generation is available on the Scale plan only. On Free and Pro plans, work through the screening framework manually.
</Note>

## Step 6 — Ask clarifying questions

Use the **Q&A** tool to ask the entrepreneur directly. Keep questions focused — one to three questions per round is usually appropriate.

<Tip>
Use AngelAI to help you frame questions. Ask it: "What questions should I ask an entrepreneur in [sector] about their go-to-market strategy?"
</Tip>

## Step 7 — Make your decision

**Move to Pitching** — the application is sufficiently developed and the company is ready to present. You will be prompted to assign a Project Lead.

**Decline** — click **Decline** in the project menu and record a brief reason. The entrepreneur is not automatically notified.

**Request more information** — continue the Q&A process and leave the project in Screening until you have what you need.
