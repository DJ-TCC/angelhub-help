## The Assessor's job

The Assessor is the first person to review an entrepreneur's application after it is submitted. Their job is to decide whether a company is ready to pitch to the angel group — not whether to invest in it.

This is an important distinction. The Assessor is not making an investment recommendation. They are acting as a quality filter: is this application complete, coherent, and sufficiently developed to be worth the group's time at a pitch event?

## When Assessors are active

Assessors work across three pipeline stages:

- **Created** — the application has just been submitted and is awaiting initial review
- **Screening** — the Assessor is actively reviewing the application
- **Pitching** — the company has been invited to pitch; the Assessor may continue to support the process before handing over to a Project Lead

Once a project moves to Due Diligence, the Assessor's role ends. The Project Lead takes over from that point.

## What Assessors can see and do

In AngelHub, Assessors can:

- View all applications in the Created, Screening, and Pitching stages
- Review the entrepreneur's submission in full
- Use the screening framework (8 dimensions) to assess readiness
- Ask the entrepreneur clarifying questions via the Q&A tool
- Generate a lead review of the submission using AngelAI (Scale plan)
- Move an application from Created to Screening, or from Screening to Pitching
- Decline an application at any stage

Assessors cannot:

- Access projects in the Due Diligence, Closing, or Completed stages (unless they are also assigned as an angel member on that deal)
- Change group settings or manage members
- Access billing information

## The Assessor and AngelAI

AngelAI is available to Assessors during the screening process. You can ask it questions about the application — for example, how to assess a particular business model, what red flags to look for in a specific sector, or how to frame a question to the entrepreneur.

AngelAI draws on the AngelHub knowledge base, which includes screening frameworks, sector guides, and red flag references. It is a thinking tool, not a decision-maker — the assessment is yours.

## Typical Assessor workflow

1. Receive a notification that a new application has been submitted
2. Open the application and read the entrepreneur's submission
3. Move the application to Screening to signal you are reviewing it
4. Work through the [screening framework](/assessor/screening-framework)
5. Ask the entrepreneur any clarifying questions needed
6. Either move to Pitching (ready to pitch) or decline with a recorded reason

See [Reviewing an application](/assessor/reviewing-an-application) for the step-by-step process.
